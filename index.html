How to Install

These apps come straight from here — not from an app store. It takes about a minute.

  1. Tap Download on the app you want. The file saves to your phone.
  2. Open the file. Pull down your notifications or open your Downloads folder and tap it.
  3. Allow "Install unknown apps" when Android asks. This just means "yes, I trust this file." It's a one-time thing.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Grant the permissions it asks for so its features work properly.
