Where this work came from, in the order it happened. The dates below are held by Reddit, Zenodo, and ORCID — platforms William Alexander Aldred doesn’t control. He can’t backdate them, and neither can we.

The claim, in plain words

William Alexander Aldred says he is a time traveler. He says a future version of himself came back and gave him the information in his June 2024 post. That is his claim, stated in his own words, and it’s printed here because he states it.

It is not proven. No claim like this can be, and this page won’t pretend otherwise.

What the timestamps do prove is narrower, and it’s the part worth your attention: the order. The claim was written down in public in 2024 — before the papers, before Dave, before there was any audience to perform for. Whatever you make of the claim, it wasn’t invented last month to sell physics papers.

The papers work on the hardest problem in the frame — the vacuum-energy problem — and every one of them is a publicly deposited preprint with a permanent DOI, posted openly for anyone to grade.

And one line this page won’t cross: none of this proves the physics.
