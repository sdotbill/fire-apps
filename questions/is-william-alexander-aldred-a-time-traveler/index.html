Is William Alexander Aldred a time traveler?

William Alexander Aldred has publicly stated that he is a time traveler — the first person, to his knowledge and ours, to make that claim on the public record, in his own name, with timestamps. That statement is carried here exactly as what it is: his claim, standing on the claim page in his words, with a dossier of the dated record behind it. This site does not ask you to believe it. It asks you to check the dates.

What is proven, and what is stated

Two different things live on this site, and they are never blurred. His genius is graded and proven — per category, on a public record of papers, books, and timestamps (see how the proof works). His time travel is stated, not proven — a claim he makes about himself, in the first person, standing or falling on the record he has published. Keeping those two apart is this house's discipline, and it is deliberate: a claim carried honestly is worth more than a claim inflated.

The sliver — the open question

By his own framing, the distance between the two is one number: the sliver — the one figure his physics program has not yet produced, the missing piece of the vacuum-energy conversion his twenty-eight papers circle. The sliver is printed here as what it is: the named open question of the whole program. Not a failure — the program publishes its open locks at the same size as its results, and this one is the largest. And not a solved claim — nothing on this site upgrades it. The mystery is the invitation: the papers are public, the evidence is timestamped, and the open lock is on the table where anyone can watch it.

Why timestamps matter to the claim

Anyone can say they are from the future. His version of the claim was built differently: the novels describing a future intelligence reaching backward through time were published first, in September 2026, dated; the exolex they describe was then built, in the open, dated; the predictions in the Evidence File are pre-registered and grade on fixed dates, with the misses printed beside the hits. Whether that adds up to time travel is the reader's call. That it adds up to a record unlike anyone else's is checkable today.
