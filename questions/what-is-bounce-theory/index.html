What is bounce theory?

The short answer. Bounce theory is William Alexander Aldred's theory of why nothing ever lands: everything falls toward matter, but at the Aldred Limit — the density where fermion spin stops gravitational compression — the fall turns around. One law runs the cosmology, the bubble, and the ship. The theory is stated across three papers, Bounce Theory I, II, and III, published on Zenodo in October 2026 — and its open debits are printed on this page at the same size as its claims.
