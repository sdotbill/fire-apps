The Surviving Count in a Torsion-Regulated Vacuum

by William Alexander Aldred — Independent Researcher

Research article · November 9, 2026 · Zenodo · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24076646 · Submitted to Gravitation and Cosmology, November 9, 2026 · Continuation of “The Vacuum Density in the Bounce Frame” (November 6, 2026)

The question

The companion paper dissolved the vacuum catastrophe's magnitude: inside the Einstein–Cartan bounce frame, the zero-point sum saturates at the derived density ρA, and the famous 10122 gap never arises. That dissolution is established, conditional on the frame's counting-saturation postulate. What survived it is not a magnitude but a count. Reducing the saturated density by the frame's binary composition, ρA/2N, returns the measured vacuum density only at N* = log2(ρA/ρΛ) = 281.1079 — and the theory's own constructions return 287, 270 and 243. A count is not dissolved by an argument about addition. It must be produced, or it stands as a measured gap.

This paper is the report of the attempt to produce it: a new construction, the executed censuses the attempt required, seven constructions of the one law the theory lacks, and a proof of exactly where — and how large — the remaining absence is. The headline stands at full size: the surviving count is not produced.

The construction

The pairing dynamics of the earlier work returns the required total T = 281 exactly when the deposit layer's capacity stands in one of four narrow intervals — so a production is a thickness, determined to within one cell by a statement of the theory. Reading a thickness off a census of available lengths had already closed empty. This paper instead derives the thickness from the layer's own behaviour: the standing layer is stratified by age, material settling along the layer's equation-of-state trajectory as later deposits bury it, so the layer's capacity is its settling age. Under the settling law dx/da = r·x(1−x), the age is A* = ln(410A)/r, and the thickness follows. The construction is honest about what it returns: not a number but a function of two inputs the theory does not yet supply — the settling rate r, equivalently the layer's settling time τs against the crossing period τx, and the initial density at deposition. At the illustrative initial density 0.01, production requires a settling time of six to ten crossing periods. The paper then asks the inventory, exhaustively, whether it holds either input.

The censuses

The censuses were executed as uniform computations, not assembled by inspection — the pairing dynamics re-implemented and gated on reproducing its registered evaluations exactly, the four intervals re-derived rather than copied, arithmetic at 50-digit precision. Every timescale of the theory as a candidate settling time: the admissible identifications return settling ages of at most 18.28 crossings against intervals that begin at 70, and each is refuted as a production by its own totals. Every ratio the timescales generate: three ratios return T = 281 exactly — and each is exhibited with its defect, that the settling time it implies is not a timescale of the theory and the initial density at which it lands is not derived. Every candidate initial density: each is a quantity of another kind spent as a density, and the one deposit quantity the theory owns is an energy, whose conversion to a density needs the deposition volume — the deposit's transverse extent, which is not available. Every ratio of the companion second-clock dynamics: exactly one combination lands in a band, and no statement of the theory makes any process run on the sum of two processes' periods. The censuses close the space of quantities that could complete the construction from the existing inventory — and they localise the obstruction to a single missing statement.

The kinetic law it lacks

Every intrinsic rate is at least 3.8× too fast — for a structural reason

The layer material's dynamical time and the crossing period are the same time, to within the theory's available factors. Every rate built from the theory's own constants and lengths is therefore of order one per crossing, while the intervals require a rate six to ten times slower. The gap is empty of admissible statements from both sides.

Seven constructions of the missing law from the theory's own materials are reported: a replay of the bulk's trajectory kinetics (refuted by computation); the instability rate at the stratum's own wavenumber floor (refuted — the material's only intrinsic rate, at its slowest admissible wavenumber, remains five to twenty times too fast); a transport family, which converts the obstruction into a missing mean free path of about one thickness unit; the gravitational timescales, which exhibit the structural reason; a hydrostatic profile, which holds less than half a cell — the standing layer is held by the deposit bookkeeping, not by its own hydrostatics; a fixed point of the layer's own thickness, the one construction returning ages in range, which at one boundary-condition convention returns A* = 85.68 and the total 280, 0.32 cells below the principal interval — refused, because the convention that selects it is not fixed by any statement of the theory, and the spread across conventions straddles every interval; and a wall-time fixed point, which has no finite solution. The absence is then proved at inventory scope: within the theory's present statements — its axioms, its constitutive assumption, and the closed censuses — no kinetic law of the layer's density trajectory is derivable. The paper is explicit about the scope: this is a statement about the current inventory, not a proof that such a law cannot exist in the theory.

The missing premise, priced in advance

The minimal premise that would supply the law is one constitutive sentence about the layer material, and the paper denominates it in advance in three equivalent forms, each with the size the intervals require: a mean free path λ/ℓT ∈ [0.772, 0.959) at initial density 0.01; a settling duration τs/τx ∈ [6.60, 8.20) at the same density; or a floor constant K0 ∈ [293.6, 300.1] fixing how the layer's longest mode is terminated at its faces. All three descend from one object — the deposit layer's transverse structure — and all three routes meet at the programme's stated next problem: the transverse geometry of the cut as the connection supplies it. The premise cannot arrive unmeasured: its required size is printed before it exists, and the gated pairing dynamics will grade whatever supplies it, in public, under falsifiers registered in the paper before the computations were performed. One disclosure closes the paper: under a weaker provenance standard, the three exact ratios of the census read as an agreement of 0.1079 counts with the required count. This programme's stated standard does not admit them, the counts are not claimed — and the alternative reading is printed, with every number needed to grade it, rather than suppressed.

Falsifiers

  • F1 (production). A derivation, registered before its computation and having neither the required count nor the intervals among its inputs, of a kinetic law, a settling time, or a statement about the layer's transverse structure that places the capacity in one of the four intervals — on F1 the count is produced and the debt re-denominates to the residual −0.1079.
  • F2 (downward closure). A derivation of the layer's thickness or capacity outside all four intervals closes the thickness route, its miss printed at the same size as any success.
  • F3 (law-form dependence). If a derived total changes under a parameter-free change of the settling law's form, the construction survives only as a near-miss exhibit. This criterion has already fired at the parametric level for the law family as presently constituted; the construction is accordingly presented as a reduction of the problem, not a live production candidate in its present form.
  • F4 (the no-go, by exhibition). The inventory no-go falls to a single exhibited derivation of a kinetic law of the layer's trajectory from the inventory it names. No further condition attaches.
  • F5 (the denominated premise). Any statement that supplies the missing premise in one of its three denominated forms is graded by the gated pairing dynamics in public; its returned total decides, under F1 or under F2.
  • F6 (the second clock and the refusals). An available process at the cut whose single period stands in one of the second clock's bands overturns that closure; a derivation of any refused implied settling time as a timescale of the layer overturns the corresponding refusal, and its total is then graded on its merits.
