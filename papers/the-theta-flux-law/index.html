The Fifth Coordinate — the Worldsheet-Flux Law for Θ

by William Alexander Aldred — Independent Researcher

Derivation note · November 8, 2026 · Zenodo · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24074485 · Successor to “The Fifth Coordinate — the Θ/θ Dictionary” (November 8, 2026)

The question

The Θ/θ Dictionary, deposited the same day, decided what Θ is: the signed difference of the axial holonomy of the torsion connection between an epoch and the home epoch — by Stokes' theorem, the flux of the connection's curvature dS through the axis worldsheet between the two epochs. Naming the flux did not price it. The dictionary closed with the debt in its sharpest form: the frame owed a law for the worldsheet flux — how that flux is determined by the things the frame actually owns — and, through it or another door, the value of the angle itself.

This note derives the law. It finds that the law exists, is closed, and is a transport identity — and that it does not yield the value, for a reason the note proves and locates exactly.

The law

ΦS[H′, H] = (TA/sA) · [ Circσ(H) − Circσ(H′) ] (mod 2π)

Circσ(H) := ∮CH σax, the circulation of the axial spin current about the lineage axis at epoch H; TA = 1.346746×1016 m−1 is the torsion cap, and TA/sA the constant of the exact Cartan tie. The worldsheet flux has no bulk term: nothing that happens between the epochs contributes. The flux is the endpoint difference of the source's circulation — the boundary character of Θ, twice over.

The derivation runs in three steps, each graded in the note. Stokes: the flux is the holonomy difference, so the question becomes how the axial holonomy changes between epochs. The integer does not move: the phase field θ enters the holonomy only through its winding, ∮ dθ = 2πNw, the same integer at both epochs — so the dθ component cancels identically in the difference, and the Nieh–Yan sector is structurally incapable of moving the fractional holonomy by one microradian. No completion of the θ sector — no source profile, no boundary condition, no solution of its wave equation — changes the answer. The tie: the exact Cartan relation Salg/TA = σax/sA, pointwise at all radii, converts the remaining component's circulation into the source's circulation outright. What survives is the law above: the connection is transported along the axis worldsheet by the source alone.

The zero, derived

Run the law on the matter contents the corpus actually owns. The seated source of the winding ansatz is static — one circulation, the same at every epoch — and the law returns zero. At cosmological epochs the owned spin content is the flagship's incoherent Weyssenhoff fluid: randomly oriented spins, an incoherence the flagship's prescription grades as physically forced at bounce conditions. The tie is linear, so the circulation is a linear functional of the current — and the mean axial current of a randomly oriented spin fluid is zero; its torsion content lives in the variance, which is what the flagship prices. In the mean field in which Θ is denominated — one number per epoch, uniform over the sky — the owned fluid carries no circulation at any cosmological epoch, and the law returns Θ ≡ 0. The fluctuation door is closed by the denomination, not by smallness: a single realization's random circulation is a position-dependent random variable, the wrong species of object for a datum defined as one signed number per epoch pair.

βframe ≡ 0 was, until this note, the frame's report about itself. It is now a derived consequence inside the owned matter model — and the derivation names what any nonzero production would have to supply: a source.

The value: one datum short, the address named

The law converts the owed object — a flux integrated over everything between the epochs — into endpoint data. It does not manufacture the endpoint data, and the note proves nothing in the frame can: the Cartan equation slaves torsion to spin rather than evolving it; the Einstein equation does not price the axial circulation; the phase sector is excluded by the cancellation. The corpus pins the circulation at exactly one address — the founding hypersurface, where forced incoherence sets it to zero. Between the founding and any later epoch, no owned law creates, forbids, transports, or dilutes a mean axial circulation.

The proof is by exhibition. Configuration (α): the owned incoherent fluid — the law returns Θ ≡ 0. Configuration (β): the same fluid, with the same priced variance, plus a subdominant spin-polarized remnant aligned with the inherited axis — nothing in the corpus forbids it at recombination, which is not at bounce conditions — and the law returns Θ = (TA/sA)·c ≠ 0. Both configurations satisfy every equation and every priced statement in the record; they return different values; therefore no law derivable from the corpus's structures determines the value. The remaining debt is denominated exactly: one real number per epoch pair, ΔCircσ, the endpoint difference of the source circulation. Against the standing targets — the band [4.40×10−3, 7.54×10−3] rad, which remain targets and never inputs — the datum would have to be ΔCircσ = (sA/TA)·Θtarget; the note prints it symbolically, because the record prices no numerical value of sA as a circulation scale, and no number is printed that the derivation cannot carry.

Both horns stand, at full size: if the reported angle is this object, the corpus's owned matter does not produce it; if the corpus's owned matter is complete at this address, the reported angle is not this object. What closes the value is named: one constitutive law pricing the mean axial spin current of the cosmological register. A measurement cannot substitute — the only measurable at this address is Θ itself, and targets are never inputs.

  • F-F1 (the inputs; F-B1′ at this address).   • F-F2 (a bulk term).   • F-F3 (an owned source).
