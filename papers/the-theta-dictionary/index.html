The Fifth Coordinate — the Θ/θ Dictionary

by William Alexander Aldred — Independent Researcher

Derivation note · November 8, 2026 · Zenodo · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24056589 · Successor to “The Fifth Coordinate — the Bundle Construction” (November 8, 2026)

The question

The fifth-coordinate program carries two angles with separate addresses. In the torsion sector, the phase θ: a field on the circle, whose gradient is half of the torsion decomposition S = Salg + dθ. In Bounce Theory II/III, the seated twist Θ: one signed number per epoch of the lineage — the scar's twist about the axis, carried through death and rebirth unchanged, and the quantity the sky's reported rotation angles (≈ 0.3°) would be measuring.

The bundle construction, deposited the same day, proved the corpus has room for only one such object: exactly one phase field, one circle, one connection. So Θ and θ cannot be two independent angles with independent laws. The dictionary between them is a forced fork — either (a) Θ is the bundle's axial holonomy, the same object as θ's field read at slice scale rather than at the craft, or (b) Θ lives in a structure the corpus has not yet written. This note decides the fork from the corpus's own statements.

The test

Every load-bearing Θ statement in Bounce Theory I–III and every load-bearing θ statement in FRONT-A was extracted — ten of the first, six of the second — and horn (a) was tested against each in turn. A single contradiction would have graded (b) and named the unwritten structure required. None came:

  • Orientation. Θ takes its sign about the axis pointing home. A holonomy around a loop linking the axis takes its sign from exactly that orientation, by the right-hand rule. No other object in the corpus signs this way; the holonomy signs natively.
  • Uniformity. Θ is the same everywhere in the sky; a point value like χ is not. Position-independence is precisely the difference between a loop integral and a point value — which is why the dictionary names the holonomy, and why χ and Θ are two readings of one connection, not one angle anchoring another.
  • Invariance and unalterability. No process inside the arena, coupling to S pointwise, reaches the holonomy around the axis; it is unchanged by any deformation that does not thread flux through the axis worldsheet, and it glues across the sever with the fibers, as the construction already derived.
  • The boundary difference. The first paper's observable — Θ(emission slice) − Θ(observation slice), achromatic, accumulated nowhere along the trip — becomes, under (a), a flux: the integral of the connection's curvature over the strip of the axis worldsheet between the two epochs. The channel the first paper named gets its geometry.
  • Zero at our slice. Θ is zero at the home epoch by construction — and in the dictionary this is a theorem of the definition, since the difference of the holonomy at coincident epochs is identically zero. The datum is a difference of readings, in the only form in which the corpus ever denominates it.
  • One small number. Around the linking loop, the holonomy splits: an integer part, 2π times the winding number — constant down the lineage, invisible mod 2π — and a fractional part, the flux of Salg. The epoch difference cancels the integer identically. What remains is one signed small angle per epoch pair: the reports' 0.3°-scale datum is the fractional holonomy difference. The twist is “the same everywhen” and Θ(recombination) ≠ Θ(our slice) — the integer and fractional parts of one loop integral, separated.

Scope of the test, printed as the note prints it: Bounce Theory III, as deposited, terminates mid-sentence inside its §3, so the test runs on Bounce Theory II complete, Bounce Theory I's angle statements, Bounce Theory III's abstract and its eleven §1 answers, and FRONT-A. Falsifier F-D1 below is the instrument that catches any contradiction standing in the unwritten remainder.

The dictionary

Θ(H) = the signed principal value of Hol(H) − Hol(Hhome)

Hol(H) := ∮CH S (mod 2π), the axial holonomy of the torsion connection at epoch hypersurface H, for a loop CH in H linking the axis worldsheet once, oriented by the right-hand rule about the home-pointing axis. By Stokes' theorem, Θ(H) equals the flux of the connection's curvature dS through the axis worldsheet between H and the home epoch. Θ at the home epoch is zero identically.

The two readings of the one field, kept distinct: χ is the craft-scale reading — the integral of S along the craft's worldline, an open-path point value carrying the readout budget σχ ≤ 0.0667 rad (SIMULATED, NOT MEASURED). Θ is the slice-scale reading — the closed-loop case of the same integral, taken as an epoch difference. Θ is not χ at any event, and χ is not Θ at any epoch. Nor is the dictionary a production of Θ's value, or a map between Θ and any other named number: it is an identification of objects, and the corpus's discipline — an identification is not a production — is kept on its face.

What remains owed

The angle's debt is unchanged in size and sharpened in denomination. The frame owes a law for the worldsheet flux of dS between epochs — the boundary-difference channel's construction, owed since the first paper, now with its integrand and its surface named — and, through that law or another door, the value Θ(recombination), against the standing measured targets (0.342°; 0.215° ± 0.074; 0.277° ± 0.057), which remain targets and never inputs. Under the dictionary, the frame's own machinery producing exactly zero at this address is the statement that the owned connection — the static winding, with Salg linear in the spin source — carries no epoch-varying worldsheet flux. Any future production must add exactly that, and nothing vaguer will count.

Falsifiers

  • F-D1 (a corpus statement against the form). Any statement of the corpus — including any in the unwritten remainder of Bounce Theory III — that seats Θ in a local field value, requires Θ to vary with position in the sky, or makes Θ a property drawn or re-drawn at a cut, kills the identification: the holonomy is none of those things.
  • F-D2 (the constructed zero). Any corpus statement requiring a measured, non-constructed Θ at our own slice — a datum rather than a definition — contradicts the dictionary, under which the difference at coincident epochs is identically zero.
  • F-D3 (accumulation, empirical). A measured rotation that accumulates along the trip — frequency-dependent, or growing with path length or source distance — kills the boundary-difference form and this dictionary with it, since the worldsheet flux depends on the endpoint hypersurfaces alone. The dictionary is hostage to the same sky that prices the targets.
