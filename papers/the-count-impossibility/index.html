The question

The companion work dissolved the vacuum catastrophe's magnitude: inside the Einstein–Cartan bounce frame, the zero-point sum saturates at the derived density ρA, and the famous 10122 gap never arises. That dissolution is established, and this document neither re-argues it nor borrows from it. What survived it is a count: reducing the saturated density by the frame's binary composition, ρA/2N, returns the measured vacuum density only at N* = log2(ρA/ρΛ) = 281.1079 — and the frame's own constructions return 287, 270 and 243. The count paper reported the attempt to produce that count and proved the absence of its kinetic law at inventory scope. The vacuum-count road then prosecuted the remaining holes one by one — fourteen cases in all. This document assembles them into a single statement.

The theorem

Production of the surviving count is impossible from the frame's owned statements — at inventory tier

Every road the programme has denominated for producing the count — fourteen cases — is closed by an executed verdict, and the two successor shapes the programme constructed to escape the inventory fail inside their own statements, structurally, at every setting of their dials. What production requires is denominated exactly: one new physical statement, of a named species, carrying named returns. The scope of the word “every” in this statement is printed at full size below: the proof quantifies over the denominated roads and the two constructed successor shapes, and over nothing else.

“Owned” means standing in the frame's settled inventory: its axioms, its constitutive sentence, consequences derived from those at theorem standard, and its closed censuses. “Inventory tier” means the proof quantifies over that present inventory — over the censuses and computations that exhibit what the inventory contains — rather than over the frame under arbitrary extension. Inventory-tier impossibility is a statement about the record: proved absence from the current statements, not inconsistency of the frame.

Scope — printed at full size

A wall map, not a metaphysical claim

The proof quantifies over the roads the programme has denominated, and over the two successor shapes the programme has constructed. It quantifies over nothing else. A road nobody has denominated is not excluded by this document. A construction species no door has named, a statement about the layer in a vocabulary the road has not used, a successor shape different in kind from the two constructed — any of these could, for all this assembly proves, produce the count tomorrow, and nothing here would stand in its way except the landing line and the falsifiers, which grade arrivals of any species.

The proof maps, with its computations exhibited, exactly where the frame's owned statements run out: which walls are censuses (complete over a named space), which are computations (executed, with their numbers), which are refusals on provenance (the species discipline, a premise of the programme, not a fact of nature), and which are structural (a scale-free exterior; an algebraic torsion; a monotone propagator). It does not claim the count cannot be produced. It claims the count cannot be produced from these statements, by these roads — and it prints the exact size and shape of the one statement whose addition would change that. The stronger endpoint — a proof that production is impossible in the frame under any extension — is not claimed, because at the standard the word proof carries in this programme it is not available.
