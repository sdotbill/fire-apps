A Fourier-Transform Spectral Observatory Measuring Microwave Background Distortions Left by a Bounce Thermal History

William Alexander Aldred · November 11, 2026

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24142254 This paper presents a space mission design, computed from stated assumptions and not measured, built to measure one quantity: the chemical-potential distortion predicted by the bounce thermal history used as the design case, for which the design case adopts 2.20e-08 in dimensionless mu distortion. The instrument is an absolute Fourier-transform spectrometer comparing the sky against an internal blackbody calibrator held at the sky temperature, covering 30 gigahertz to 3 terahertz with detectors at 0.1 kelvin, operated from a halo orbit so the instrument views cold space continuously. It operates from a halo orbit about the Sun-Earth L2 point for 4 years.
