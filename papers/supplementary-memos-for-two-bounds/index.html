About this deposit

Supplementary memos cited by the paper “Two Bounds and an Integration Constant: The Kopczyński–Trautman pin, the de Sitter floor, and the open value problem” (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23485358): the pin calibration memo, the Route E (vacuum attack) design memo, and the two-clock stress test memo.

Program note

These memos supplement “Two Bounds and an Integration Constant”, the second paper in a three-paper program on the vacuum-energy problem. The deposit collects the three memos that note cites: the pin calibration memo for the Kopczyński–Trautman pin of “The Aldred Limit” (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23485325), the Route E (vacuum attack) design memo, and the two-clock stress test memo. The program's third paper is “The Constant Changes Address” (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23610897). These memos are working documents of the program; they state no result beyond the papers they support.
