The papers of William Alexander Aldred

Fifteen physics papers, published with permanent DOIs, on the vacuum-energy problem — the 122-orders-of-magnitude gap between the vacuum energy quantum theory predicts and the value cosmology observes — attacked inside Einstein–Cartan gravity, where the spin of matter twists spacetime itself.

The program's spine is the Aldred Limit: a pinned density at which fermion spin caps gravitational compression, replacing singularities with bounces. Around it, the papers map what the frame can prove, what it cannot, and exactly where the remaining problem lives. Nothing here claims a solved cosmological constant problem — the papers grade themselves, in print.

Jorge Goncalves Cardoso replied to the author's arXiv endorsement request on October 25, 2026. Ten further papers from this program were published on October 27, 2026, and are listed below; each has its page here.
