The Consequence Tree of the Aldred Limit

by William Alexander Aldred — Independent Researcher

Paper · November 8, 2026 · Zenodo · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24056645 · Root paper: The Aldred Limit (under review at Gravitation and Cosmology)

What this paper is

The Aldred Limit — the Einstein–Cartan spin–density bound ρA ≈ 1055 g cm−3, at which fermion spin caps gravitational compression — is the root of a research program whose results are scattered across bounce cosmology, the vacuum-density problem, the large-angle microwave sky, and a navigation theory built on a fifth coordinate. Until now those results lived in separate papers, each grading its own claims. This paper assembles them into one consequence tree: every claim the program rests on the Limit, stated in dependency order, with the derivation step shown, a grade — DERIVED, CONSTRAINED, or NOT DERIVED — and the observation or computation that would falsify it.

The discipline is that a branch is carried only where the Limit actually carries the weight. Where a branch needs an added premise — a postulate, a scar, an identification — the premise is named at the joint where it enters. Where the Limit does not support a branch, the branch is printed NOT DERIVED at full size. Nothing is padded.

The root

Five statements, all from the flagship paper. The replacement: eliminating torsion for a Weyssenhoff spin fluid gives ρeff = ρ − κ²⟨S²⟩. The scaling law: the bound is the Planck density suppressed by the squared carrier-to-Planck mass ratio, ρA = ρPl(m/mPl)²/(8π·coef). The pinned value: for incoherent spins (coef = 1/8) and neutron matter, ρA = 9.72×1054 g cm−3. The prescription verdict: incoherence is physically forced at bounce conditions — every alignment channel fails by orders of magnitude. The twist form: the same bound read through the Cartan equation caps torsion at TA ≈ 1.35×1016 m−1. Two fences stand at the root: the pinned value is a property of the dust spin-fluid model (the degeneracy-complete energy density moves the balance to the Planck neighbourhood), and the author's earlier entropy form admits no map to the bound — one calibration, never a derivation.

Three consequences derived for the first time

The carrier law, evaluated

The scaling law makes the bound a pure m² in the carrier mass. Evaluated for non-neutron matter, the bounce altitude becomes a computed property of the collapsing matter's composition: an electron spin fluid turns at 2.88×1048 g cm−3, a 0.1 eV neutrino fluid at 1.10×1035 g cm−3. For mixtures the bound is a surface in composition space. The Limit does not name one bounce density for the universe; it names one per matter content. DERIVED.

The turnover identity

In the dust register, the effective density peaks at ρA/2 — and the null-energy combination reverses sign at exactly ρA/2. The two papers that print these facts never printed why they coincide: one factor, 1 − 2ρ/ρA, controls both. The density at which the gravitating source stops growing is the density at which the singularity theorems' energy-condition premise fails. DERIVED.

The epoch-pair corollary

The Θ dictionary (November 8, 2026) identifies the sky's rotation angle as an epoch difference of the axial holonomy. It follows that a polarization rotation is a property of an epoch pair, not a universal constant: rotations measured against two different emission hypersurfaces differ by exactly the worldsheet flux between them, and agree only if that flux vanishes. An analysis that combines rotation measurements from different emission epochs into a single angle assumes the vanishing it should be testing. DERIVED.

The empty leaves, at full size

A consequence tree that printed only its derivations would be a poster. This one prints its empty leaves at the same size, because they are the program's work list. The vacuum count: landing on the measured density requires N* = 281.1079; the closest owned count misses by 5.8921 counts, the census of owned lengths has closed empty, and the missing object — an owned deposit-layer thickness in four named windows, or a second clock — does not exist in the record. The residue factor 1.027500: produced by nothing. The angle's value: the frame's own machinery produces exactly zero rotation; what is owed is a law for the worldsheet flux between epochs. The founding profile's shape: the marking law is unowned. The readout: the fifth coordinate's measurand is defined and no instrument reads it. Each empty leaf names the single object that would fill it, and the falsifier that would close its branch instead.

And the branches that could be executed against the world have been: a stellar null confirmed to forty orders of magnitude; a Cold Spot account executed to failure by its own pre-registered falsifier on the Planck sky (the Equatorial Witness); an axis kill line standing open for want of a wide enough galaxy catalogue. The shape of the tree is the result: from one root, the derivations run clean for two levels — and then, at every branch, meet one named number or law the frame does not yet own.
