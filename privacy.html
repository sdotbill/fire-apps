Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy covers the apps published by Premium Marketing LLC: Forge Studio, Codex Press, and Forge. Plain version: your data stays on your phone. We don't sell anything, we don't run ads SDKs, and we don't track you.

Forge Studio

  • On your device only: your Gemini API key, app settings, saved scripts, avatar images, and podcast projects are stored on your phone and never sent to us.
  • Gemini features: script writing, avatar image generation, and video prompts are sent to Google's Gemini API so the features work. That's Google's service — their privacy policy applies to that traffic.
  • Speech: episode voiceover uses your phone's built-in speech services.
  • Crash logs: if the app crashes, a log file is saved to your Downloads folder so you can send it to us. It never uploads itself.

📚 Codex Press

  • On your device only: your Google Drive sign-in token, Gemini API key, and per-book publishing profiles are stored on your phone.
  • Google Drive: the app reads your Drive file names (read-only) to show your book packages. It cannot edit or delete your files.
  • Gemini AI help: questions you ask the in-app assistant go to Google's Gemini API.
  • Amazon/KDP: the app never logs into Amazon or KDP and never touches your Amazon credentials. You do the final upload tap yourself.

🔨 Forge

  • Your accounts, your keys: Forge runs in your browser. The PayPal and Google credentials you connect are yours alone — they are stored by your browser and sent only to PayPal and Google.
  • Your business data: clients, prospects, posts, and payment records you create stay in your browser and your own connected services. We don't see or store them.

What we never do

  • No accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone.
  • API keys you paste into Settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach Premium Marketing LLC through the contact details on the Premium Marketing website.
