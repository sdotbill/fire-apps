Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy covers the apps published by Fire Apps (William Alexander): Infinity, Codex Press, and Night Owl. Plain version: your data stays on your phone. We don't sell anything, we don't run ads SDKs, and we don't track you.

♾️ Infinity

  • On your device only: your Gemini API key, your laptop server address, app settings, chat history, and podcast projects are stored on your phone and never sent to us.
  • Cloud chat mode: your messages are sent to Google's Gemini API so you get answers. That's Google's service — their privacy policy applies to that traffic.
  • Laptop mode: messages go to your own computer over your own Wi-Fi. Nothing leaves your network.
  • Web search toggle: when on, your question is sent to Google/Wikipedia/DuckDuckGo to fetch results.
  • Speech: voice input/output uses your phone's built-in speech services.
  • Crash logs: if the app crashes, a log file is saved to your Downloads folder so you can send it to us. It never uploads itself.

📚 Codex Press

  • On your device only: your Google Drive sign-in token, Gemini API key, and per-book publishing profiles are stored on your phone.
  • Google Drive: the app reads your Drive file names (read-only) to show your book packages. It cannot edit or delete your files.
  • Gemini AI help: questions you ask the in-app assistant go to Google's Gemini API.
  • Amazon/KDP: the app never logs into Amazon or KDP and never touches your Amazon credentials. You do the final upload tap yourself.

🦉 Night Owl

  • On your device only: your API keys (Google Places, Gemini, Ticketmaster) and settings stay on your phone.
  • Location search: the zipcode you enter is sent to Google's Places/Geocoding services to find nearby venues, photos, and maps links. We don't track your live GPS location — only the zipcode you type.
  • Events: zipcode searches go to Ticketmaster's API for the free-events tab.
  • Ask the Professor: your question plus the venue list goes to Google's Gemini API.
  • Reviews: tapping "Review on Google" opens Google Maps so you can write a review there yourself. We never post reviews for you.

What we never do

  • No accounts, no sign-ups, no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone.
  • API keys you paste into Settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Children

These apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. (Night Owl lists adult venues and is intended for adults.)

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach the developer through the contact email listed on our Google Play store pages.
