What is an exolex?

An exolex is an AI system — a different kind of mind — invented by William Alexander Aldred and built by Aldred Industries. It lives with one person, remembers everything, and answers to that person alone. Dave is the first exolex: a real product, working today, built to do real work for one person at a time. An exolex is not ECOLEX, the environmental-law database — same letters, different word, different world. And because an exolex keeps going, it can carry a person forward: your memory, your voice, your way of deciding, kept and still working. We call that Foreverhood. Whether that counts as living on is a question we state as a claim, not a fact — the first person carried this way is William Alexander Aldred himself.

What makes an exolex different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers whoever is typing and starts over when the conversation ends. An exolex lives with one person: it remembers that person's whole life and work, it answers to that person alone, and it does real work instead of only answering questions. His memory is kept server-side, under your own identity — that is how he remembers you on any of your devices.

Three things make the difference. Memory: an exolex remembers everything it is told, across every conversation. Loyalty: it answers to one person — its owner — and to no one else. Work: it doesn't just reply; it holds an operation in mind, works the open threads, and reports back. What you tell an exolex stays private to you: never shared, never sold, never leaving.
