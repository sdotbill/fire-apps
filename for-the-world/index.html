For the World

Aldred Industries exists to solve the world's problems — and to give the solutions away in the open. Every design on this page was worked out from first principles and published with a permanent DOI, where anyone in the world can read it, check it, and learn from it: clean water for villages, oxygen for clinics that cylinders cannot reach, power and storage for homes, food kept safe, buildings that stand, and instruments whose numbers can be trusted.

The model is simple. Aldred Industries does not manufacture. It solves the problem and publishes the design; the publication, with its DOI and its date, is the public record of the work. Manufacturers license the designs and build them. What is sold is the licence — the knowledge itself is given.

An honest note on what these designs are

Every design in this catalogue is a publicly deposited blueprint — simulation-grade work: simulated, not measured, and not built. The numbers inside each design come from its own deposited computation, and each paper prints its limits as plainly as its results. A licence conveys the design record and the right to develop it; certification applies once a build exists to test. Designs in this catalogue are licensed to manufacturers by Aldred Industries; the few that are not offered say so on their cards.

Licence a design

A licence is the right to build and sell a design. Aldred Industries licenses its designs; it does not manufacture them. What a licence conveys is the design record itself — the design paper and, where one exists, its blueprint package, each publicly deposited with a permanent DOI — together with the right to develop that design into a product. Certification applies once a build exists to test.

Two licence shapes are offered:

Single-field licence

The right to build and sell the design in one named field of use — one application, one market. The design remains available for licence in other fields.

Full licence

The right to build and sell the design across fields of use, as one whole-design licence.

Licence terms are set on inquiry. Use the button on any design's card — it opens an email already addressed to us, naming that design and its DOI. Say which licence shape you are interested in and what you would build, and the terms for that design come back in reply. The designs that are not offered for licence say so on their cards.
