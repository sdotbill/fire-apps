Aldred Industries exists to solve the world's problems — and to give the solutions away in the open. Every design on this page was worked out from first principles and published with a permanent DOI, where anyone in the world can read it, check it, and learn from it: clean water for villages, oxygen for clinics that cylinders cannot reach, power and storage for homes, food kept safe, buildings that stand, and instruments whose numbers can be trusted.

The model is simple. Aldred Industries does not manufacture. It solves the problem and publishes the design; the publication, with its DOI and its date, is the public record of the work. Manufacturers license the designs and build them. What is sold is the licence — the knowledge itself is given.

Optical Depth Required for Efficient Electromagnetically Induced Transparency Photon Storage

Storing a photon as a collective excitation in an atomic ensemble succeeds with a probability that rises with optical depth and falls with decoherence during the storage interval. Optical depth is therefore the first design number for an electromagnetically induced transparency memory.

Bandwidth and Noise Budget for an Off-Resonant Raman Single-Photon Memory

An off-resonant Raman memory trades optical depth and control energy for bandwidth: it can accept short photons that a narrowband memory cannot, at the price of a noise floor set by spontaneous scattering from the control field. This study computes the quantities that follow from stated assumptions for a warm caesium vapour Raman memory, photon duration 1 ns, detuning 1 to 5 GHz, control energies spanning an order of magnitude, using the relations set out in the Methods.
