Aldred Industries exists to solve the world's problems — and to give the solutions away in the open. Every design on this page was worked out from first principles and published with a permanent DOI, where anyone in the world can read it, check it, and learn from it: clean water for villages, oxygen for clinics that cylinders cannot reach, power and storage for homes, food kept safe, buildings that stand, and instruments whose numbers can be trusted.

The model is simple. Aldred Industries does not manufacture. It solves the problem and publishes the design; the publication, with its DOI and its date, is the public record of the work. Manufacturers license the designs and build them. What is sold is the licence — the knowledge itself is given.

Household Biogas from the Waste a Family Actually Has: An Honest Energy Budget and a Digester Design for a Six-Person Household with Three Cattle

This blueprint specifies a household biogas digester for one stated household: - 6 people; - 3 zebu-type cattle, free-grazing by day and kraaled at night; - 2 goats, whose dung is excluded from the feedstock because grazing goats scatter hard pellets that are not practically collectable in useful quantity; - a warm lowland climate, with unheated slurry temperature ranging from 19 °C in the coldest months to 29 °C in the warmest, and cold spells reaching 17 °C. The design is sized backwards from the waste this household actually produces, not from the cooking demand.

A Spin-Polarised Thermionic Converter for High-Temperature Heat

The device examined here is a spin-polarised thermionic converter intended for converting concentrated high-temperature heat directly to direct-current power without moving parts.
