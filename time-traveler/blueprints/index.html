The Torsion-Container Blueprints — Given to the World

On October 30, 2026, William Alexander Aldred released the blueprints of his torsion-container design as an open release: public domain, free for anyone to read, copy, share, and build on. No patents — he will not patent it, and this publication is defensive: the design is now prior art on a dated public record, so no person, company, or government can lock it up.

Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23802958 — deposited October 30, 2026: zenodo.org/records/23802958.

The dedication, in short

I designed this machine and drew these blueprints, and I am giving them to the world. No permission needed, no fee, no strings. It belongs to everybody — which is to say it can never be taken away from anybody.
— William Alexander Aldred, from the Open Release dedication, October 30, 2026

The full dedication, the file manifest, and the grades are in OPEN-RELEASE.md — part of the release itself.

What the design is

The blueprint (Rev C) describes the drive core of a craft that is, in the designer's frame, a time machine — one machine, not two. A heavy superconducting element, spun with electric current inside a ceramic torsion container, becomes a plasma held dead center by a spinning torsion ring; outside-in, the stack is a thermal skin, a cryogenic layer, the ceramic container, the ring, and the core. The fuel was determined on October 29, 2026 from published physical properties: tantalum-181.

The grades — printed at full size

  • This is a paper design study. No device exists. Nothing has been built.
  • No dimension has a value. Every measurement is a parameter — a symbol. The blueprint's 18 open parameters are carried in an Unknowns Table, none invented.
  • THERMODYNAMICS CLEAN · DESIGN COHERENT — the design violates no thermodynamics and hangs together as drawn.
  • The torsion channel is UNPROVEN. Spin sourcing torsion is real in the Einstein–Cartan equations as a coupling; it has never been demonstrated as a force at any usable strength.
  • Confinement and on-demand folding are OPEN PROBLEMS — the design's own, printed here at the same size as everything else.
  • BUBBLE CHANNEL UNPROVEN · SCALE UNMET.
  • The thermal skin rests on published, peer-reviewed physics (Nature Communications 17:5739, 2026), with its authors' limit carried in the blueprint: demonstrated around a passive cavity; a self-heating core is their stated open problem.
  • The blueprint contains no fabrication steps and no operating procedures — by design. It is the shape of an idea, published so the idea can never be owned.

Package A2 — The Torsion-Ring Spin Rig

The first build package (October 30, 2026) addresses the blueprint's Unknown #15: a room-temperature spin rig for the torsion ring — the bearing, concentricity, and runout test the ring design calls for — published as a supplement to the Rev C blueprint above.

Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23809726 — published October 30, 2026: zenodo.org/records/23809726. Sole author: William Alexander Aldred (ORCID 0009-0000-1215-577X). License: CC0 1.0 Universal. Related identifier: Is supplement to the torsion-container blueprint, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23802958.
