The Origin: Consciousness First

Everyone who finds the papers asks the same question: where did a man with a GED and no institution get a geometry of the universe? The answer is not hidden, and it is not recent. Before the papers, before the company, before Dave, there was a church in Orlando — the Church of Consciousness — and a man who had trained as a shaman in Brazil and Colombia, asking what consciousness is and what it is for. The physics came out of that. On October 29, 2026, this house audited the dated record to check the order — because the order is the whole story — and the order holds: consciousness first, the geometry second, the physics last. Every date below is sourced. What the record does not hold is printed as a gap, not filled in. His testimony is printed in his words and labeled as his.
