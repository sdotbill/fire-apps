The Discipline

Sealed first. Scored in public.

Every call the desk makes is written down and sealed — timestamped, hash-stamped, and uneditable — before its event or window opens. Afterward it is graded against the actual result and printed in the public record, hits and misses at the same size. A call that cannot be graded on its named source stays open with the reason printed. The desk never wagers, trades, or moves money; the lines and prices it reads are information only. That discipline — not a slogan — is the product.
