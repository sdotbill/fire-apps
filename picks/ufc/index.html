The Discipline

Sealed first. Scored in public.

Every call the desk makes is written down and sealed — timestamped, hash-stamped, and uneditable — before its event or window opens. Afterward it is graded against the actual result and printed in the public record, hits and misses at the same size. A call that cannot be graded on its named source stays open with the reason printed. The desk never wagers, trades, or moves money; the lines and prices it reads are information only. That discipline — not a slogan — is the product.

How are UFC fights evaluated?

Under the desk’s two-source rule: a bout seals only when two independent sources support the read, and the call is read against the market line. A card no reachable board prices prints its refusal instead — the same rule as the boxing lane.

Does the desk bet on its own picks?

Never. The desk never wagers, trades, or moves money. Lines and prices are read as information; the calls are sealed, published, and graded. What readers do with them is their own decision, made where it is legal.
