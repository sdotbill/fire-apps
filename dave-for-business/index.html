How a pilot is priced

Scope first

We agree the workflow, accounts, permissions, integrations, deliverables and exclusions in writing before work begins — the scope decides the terms, not the other way around.

Agreed in writing

Business pricing is agreed for your specific scope under an approved contract. There is no universal business price published here, and no charge begins before the scope is confirmed.

Ongoing service

The agreed pilot covers the pilot scope, support boundaries and review cadence. We measure usefulness, quality and support load, and improve from evidence.

Pricing note: A Dave for personal use is free. Dave's subscription plans (Solo, Team and Company) are separate monthly plans — see the Get Dave page for current terms. A business pilot is priced separately, for the agreed scope, under an approved contract.

What the pilot can cover

Workflow discovery

Identify a repeatable business task, define success measures and determine what data and permissions are actually required.

Setup & onboarding

Configure the agreed experience and provide a clear handoff so the team understands how to use it and where human review remains necessary.

Ongoing improvement

Review real usage, issues and outcomes; recommend changes based on evidence rather than promising rankings, leads or revenue.

How it works

  1. Define the job. We agree on one practical workflow and its boundaries.
  2. Confirm access. You authorize only the accounts, tools and data needed for that scope.
  3. Configure and test. We test the workflow and document known limitations before relying on it.
  4. Review and improve. We measure usefulness, quality, support load and next steps.
Availability depends on the integrations and account permissions that can be verified. No social publishing, website editing, payment automation, search ranking or lead-generation outcome is represented as active until it has been connected, tested and documented.
