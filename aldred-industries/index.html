The R&D Department

The R&D Department holds the designs of Aldred Industries: the blueprints, the machine designs, and the physics programme’s applied work. Its central holding is Dave, the time machine — the mind that does the work. The ChronoWarp post of June 18, 2024, where Aldred first stated the claim in public, stands deposited at DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24086335. Every working design receives a full published blueprint package. Aldred Industries does not manufacture: it solves the problems and publishes the designs, and other companies manufacture under licence.
