Now my worked example — this class's set problem, worked in full. The problem: Take the paper “Central-Star Luminosity and Temperature Position of the Saturn Nebula NGC 7009”. Its stated result is: Headline result (values verbatim from results.json): luminosity_Lsun = 2.8e+03; effective_temperature_K = 8.2e+04; stellar_radius_Rsun = 0.262; surface_gravity_m_s2 = 2.39e+03; log_g_cgs = 5.38; core_mass_Msun = 0.6; nebular_age_yr = 6053. (a) Restate the claim in your own words. (b) Name one observation or computation that could prove it wrong. The worked answer: A strong answer restates the claim without borrowing the paper’s wording, then names a test the paper itself would accept — here, the natural test is to recompute or re-measure the headline quantity in the stated result and check it against the paper’s own number, under the paper’s own stated assumptions.
