Now my worked example — this class's set problem, worked in full. The problem: Take the paper “A Temperature Ceiling for Evaporating Primordial Black Holes from the Density Bound”. Its stated result is: This paper reports a computation from stated assumptions (status: simulated, not measured) on a Temperature Ceiling for Evaporating Primordial Black Holes from the Density Bound. Headline computed values: M min g = 1.000e+13; T max K = 1.227e+13; T max MeV = 1.057e+03; T Pl K = 1.417e+32. (a) Restate the claim in your own words. (b) Name one observation or computation that could prove it wrong. The worked answer: A strong answer restates the claim without borrowing the paper’s wording, then names a test the paper itself would accept — here, the natural test is to recompute or re-measure the headline quantity in the stated result and check it against the paper’s own number, under the paper’s own stated assumptions.
