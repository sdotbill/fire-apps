Now my worked example — this class's set problem, worked in full. The problem: Take the paper “Carbon Enhancement in Second-Generation Stars from Faint First Supernovae with Fallback”. Its stated result is: This study computed, from stated assumptions, the following headline results: Fe yield Msun = 0.004; C yield Msun = 0.20; dilution Msun = 30000; [Fe/H] = -4.01; [C/Fe] = 1.46; CEMP threshold [C/Fe] = 0.7. (a) Restate the claim in your own words. (b) Name one observation or computation that could prove it wrong. The worked answer: A strong answer restates the claim without borrowing the paper’s wording, then names a test the paper itself would accept — here, the natural test is to recompute or re-measure the headline quantity in the stated result and check it against the paper’s own number, under the paper’s own stated assumptions.
