Now my worked example — this class's set problem, worked in full. The problem: Take the paper “Anisotropic Birefringence: Patch-to-Patch Variance Around a Uniform Angle and What It Adds to the Mean”. Its stated result is: The headline result of this computed study is: rms at 0p05 = 0.224; rms at 0p10 = 0.316; mean deg = 0.30; patch scatter over mean pct = 75. (a) Restate the claim in your own words. (b) Name one observation or computation that could prove it wrong. The worked answer: A strong answer restates the claim without borrowing the paper’s wording, then names a test the paper itself would accept — here, the natural test is to recompute or re-measure the headline quantity in the stated result and check it against the paper’s own number, under the paper’s own stated assumptions.
