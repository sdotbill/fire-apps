Now my worked example — this class's set problem, worked in full. The problem: Take the paper “Calbuco 2015: Stratospheric Injection Fraction, Sulphur Loading and Transcontinental Ash Transport Times”. Its stated result is: Headline result: plume height km = 23; tropopause height km = 12; stratospheric overshoot km = 11; settling 20um m s = 0.0327; fall time 20um days = 6.371; fall time 10um days = 25.49 (a) Restate the claim in your own words. (b) Name one observation or computation that could prove it wrong. The worked answer: A strong answer restates the claim without borrowing the paper’s wording, then names a test the paper itself would accept — here, the natural test is to recompute or re-measure the headline quantity in the stated result and check it against the paper’s own number, under the paper’s own stated assumptions.
