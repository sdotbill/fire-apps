Now my worked example — this class's set problem, worked in full. The problem: Take the paper “The Helium-Ionisation Acoustic Glitch as an Abundance Meter: A Signal-Period and Sensitivity Calculation”. Its stated result is: This paper computes a quantitative budget for its stated stellar question from deposited, reproducible arithmetic, with headline results period 700 = 714; period 1000 = 500; dY for 10pct = 0.025. (a) Restate the claim in your own words. (b) Name one observation or computation that could prove it wrong. The worked answer: A strong answer restates the claim without borrowing the paper’s wording, then names a test the paper itself would accept — here, the natural test is to recompute or re-measure the headline quantity in the stated result and check it against the paper’s own number, under the paper’s own stated assumptions.
