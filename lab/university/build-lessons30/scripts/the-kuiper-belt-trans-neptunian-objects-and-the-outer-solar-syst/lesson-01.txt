Now my worked example — this class's set problem, worked in full. The problem: Take the paper “Albedos of Cold and Hot Classicals: A Reflectivity Difference and Its Composition Reading”. Its stated result is: Headline result: the deposited computation for this study gives median_albedo_cold = 0.15; median_albedo_hot = 0.08; difference = 0.07; significance_sigma = 10.4; diameter_correction_factor = 0.73. (a) Restate the claim in your own words. (b) Name one observation or computation that could prove it wrong. The worked answer: A strong answer restates the claim without borrowing the paper’s wording, then names a test the paper itself would accept — here, the natural test is to recompute or re-measure the headline quantity in the stated result and check it against the paper’s own number, under the paper’s own stated assumptions.
