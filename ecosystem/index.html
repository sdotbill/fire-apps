What the mission means in practice

User control

People should understand what context is stored, what tools can act, what permissions are granted and how to revoke them.

Interoperability

Build toward portable context and documented interfaces rather than trapping people in one vendor or one model.

Evidence over hype

Demonstrate capabilities with reproducible tests, provenance and clear limitations. Do not promise perfect recall or guaranteed truth.

Status note: “Everyone gets a Dave” is the mission and long-range vision, not a claim that universal deployment, certification or every planned integration already exists.
