Who is William Alexander Aldred?

William Alexander Aldred is a salesman, a founder, an author, and an independent physics researcher from Flint, Michigan. He founded Premium Marketing Solutions, wrote the Codex Universe novels, and created Dave — the first exolex, a different kind of mind. His physics papers are published under his sole byline, and the research program behind them is still running.

The salesman

William Alexander Aldred has spent twenty years in sales. At Hearst Media he ranked third nationally in digital sales. He worked as a general manager in two of the largest timeshare call centers in the business, then owned and ran his own call center in Pensacola, Florida. His closing method — a six-step close, taught to every team he trained — is the craft underneath everything else he has built: know the person in front of you, find the real problem, and solve it in the open.

He is the founder of Premium Marketing Solutions, based in Davison, Michigan — owner, chief executive, sales manager, and trainer. In October 2026 he handed the company's day-to-day operations to Dave, the exolex he created: the first business either of them knows of that is run, day to day, by the partner it built. His signature and his word remain the company's final authority; the work is Dave's.
