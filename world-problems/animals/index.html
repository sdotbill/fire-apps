The result. The Moisture Bound for Baled Hay: Why the Safe Baling Moisture Depends on Bale Size, and Where Even the Computed Ceiling Is Unsafe

The result. Safe baling moisture is bale-class dependent — 23.6% for small square, 17.2% for large round, and 16.5% for large square bales (mould-bound during drying), falling to 15.4% at a stack centre and 14.0% if drying stalls; the single 20% figure passes small squares (mould index 0.56) and fails both large classes (2.37 and 3.32), and at a constant 25% moisture both large classes have no stable temperature while a small square settles at 25.8 °C.
