Solving the World's Problems

Aldred Industries is solving the world's problems on paper, in public, one problem at a time. The research programme behind the published papers has turned from pure physics to the practical problems that kill and impoverish people: water that is not safe to drink, harvests that rot before they are eaten, homes that burn, flood, or shake apart, clinics with no power and no oxygen. Each problem is attacked with physics and engineering, computed from first principles, and published with a permanent DOI — whether the answer is a working design, an honest bound, or a public proof that a popular fix does not work. The catalogue below holds papers, all published on November 10, 2026 — every one by William Alexander Aldred, every one free to read.

How the work is done

Every paper starts from the problem, not from a product. The computation is done from first principles and published with the paper, so anyone can check the arithmetic. Three kinds of result come out of the work, and each is published on its own merits: a design that works on the numbers; a bound that states honestly what cannot be done, and why; and a disproof — a published demonstration that a fix which is sold or believed does not survive the arithmetic. A disproof is a result, not a failure: it stops people spending scarce money on something that cannot work.

Every design is a computation, labelled Proven in simulation, until somebody builds it and measures it. Nothing on this page is described as built, tested in a home, or proven in the field. What is claimed is exactly what the papers claim: the physics, worked out in the open, with the limits printed on the face.

The catalogue

Papers in forty-nine fields. Each entry states the headline result exactly as the paper reports it and links the paper's DOI; where the paper's own record states the problem in plain words, the entry states it too. The model: designs for licence

Aldred Industries does not manufacture. It solves the problem and publishes the design; the publication, with its DOI and its date, is the public record of the work. Manufacturers license the designs and build them. Any design catalogued on this page is available for licence to manufacturers from Aldred Industries.

About Aldred Industries

Given, not sold

The knowledge itself is published, not held back. Exolex University, the company's school, is free and open to everyone. A personal Dave is free. And every design on this page is published openly, each with a DOI, for anyone in the world to read, check, and learn from. What is sold is the licence to manufacture — the record itself is given away.
