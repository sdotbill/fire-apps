The sick room at home

Caring for the sick at home: fever, wounds, turning, lifting, and clean air in one room — what one pair of hands can and cannot do.

The Sick Room's Water Store: How Long Boiled Water Keeps, and the Daily Litres That Drinking, Wounds, and Hands Require

The result. Covered boiled water crosses the stated threshold after 29.9 hours at 30 °C but only 14.95 hours at 37 °C, and the sick room needs 13.8 litres per day (14 litres boiled in 2 batches, 2.292 kg wood).

Dressings from Household Cloth: Boiling Time, the Iron's Margin, and the Fuel in a Batch of Clean Dressings

The result. Boiling reaches the stated standard at all altitudes if time rises from 12.0 minutes at sea level to 60.39 minutes at 2000 m (1.285 kg wood per batch of 20), while the iron reaches it only on its high setting at 200 °C.

How Far a Cough Travels in a Sick Room: Settling, Drifting, and What the Distance Rule Rests On

The result. No droplet size reaches 2 m on cough momentum alone (about 1.2 m), but at a 0.25 m/s room current droplets of 5 to 100 µm are still airborne at 2 m.

The Cooling Budget of a Fevered Adult: How Much Heat Water, Air, and Fanning Can Remove, and What One Degree Costs

The result. One degree costs 242.9 kJ and about 0.1 litres of evaporated water; fanning wetted skin at 30 °C and 50 percent humidity gives a net 1400.8 W, or 2.9 minutes per degree at the calculated bound.

The Laundry of a Sick Room: Water, Fuel, and Rinses to a Stated Cleanliness

The result. Three rinses reach a residual fraction of 0.000751 in 60.0 litres per load, and a week of washing every second day costs 240.0 litres of water, 12.94 kg of wood if boiled, and 280 caregiver minutes.

One Pair of Hands: Whether the Full Day of Home Care Fits into Twenty-Four Hours

The result. The day fits in total hours (20.55 committed, 3.45 uncommitted) but not as sleep: strict 2-hourly turning leaves an 86-minute longest sleep block, and lengthening the night interval restores sleep only by exceeding the stated pressure limit by 24 mmHg·h (4-hour interval) or 76 mmHg·h (6-hour gap).

Padding Priced in Millimetres: Peak Pressure at Heel and Hip Against the Thickness and Softness of What Is Under Them

The result. At stated thicknesses, soft foam, firm foam, and a water-filled bag bring peak pressure below the 32 mmHg target at both heel and hip, while folded cloth (60.2 mmHg heel, 68.8 mmHg hip) and a rice-hull pad do not; minimum thickness to target ranges from 13.1 mm (water bag, heel) to 89.6 mm (folded cloth, hip).

One Room, Two People: The Openings That Give a Stated Number of Air Changes, and the Hour the Air Takes to Clear

The result. Stack effect alone at a 2.0 K difference needs 0.193 m² for 6 air changes per hour and 0.387 m² for 12, while a caregiver's 2-hour integrated concentration falls to 8.0% of the unventilated value at 12 air changes per hour.

A Rope to Sit Up By: What a Knotted Rope at the Bed End Asks of the Arms, and Whom It Cannot Help

The result. Sitting up by rope alone peaks at 0.318 of body weight at 0 degrees, so it is possible only at a both-arm pull of 0.4 of body weight, not at 0.15 or 0.25.

Sliding a Helpless Adult Across a Bed: Friction, Sheet Materials, and the Pull One Caregiver Can Sustain

The result. Only the smooth synthetic sheet on plastic (103.0 N for 70 kg) and the low-friction pair (54.9 N for 70 kg) bring a level slide within one caregiver's stated sustained pull of 150 N.

Tepid Sponging, Priced in Minutes: The Lag Between Skin and Core, and How Long the Effect Lasts

The result. A 10-minute sponging session lowers the shell by 1.87 °C but the core by only 0.1 °C, the surface effect fades within 27.8 minutes, and the core reduction reaches 0.31 °C one hour later.

Hydration by Spoonfuls: Sip Size, Interval, and the Deficit an Overnight Gap Creates

The result. Meeting a stated requirement of 2950.0 mL per day needs 10 mL every 2 minutes for 9.83 hours, an 8 hour overnight gap alone creates a deficit of 983.3 mL, and no spoon schedule suffices when only 6 offering hours are available.

The result. The governing safe load is 2408.2 N for 60 mm poles at 3.5 m (legs govern) and 2600.0 N for all other combinations (rope governs), covering a 95 kg suspended mass even with a dynamic factor of 1.5.

The result. Propped Up on Pillows: The Stack Height Behind a Stated Backrest Angle, and the Mouthful It Allows

The result. After compression, 5 pillows are needed for a 30 degree target and 6 for 45 degrees, raising cuts a 400 mL meal from 60.0 minutes when flat to 17.25 minutes, and sliding without a knee support begins at 3 pillows.

A Wet Sheet over the Window: How Much a Room Can Be Cooled by Evaporation, and When It Makes a Fever Worse

The result. At 35 °C the wet sheet helps the patient below 56 percent outdoor relative humidity and harms above it; at 70 percent humidity it cools the room by only 0.8 °C while worsening patient net heat loss by 14.7 W.

The result. At adequate pressure (bottle at 1.2 m, 11.772 kPa) a 99.9 percent reduction needs 3.0 litres in 5.29 minutes through 3.1416 square millimetres of holes, while below the threshold the same reduction needs 13.523 litres.
