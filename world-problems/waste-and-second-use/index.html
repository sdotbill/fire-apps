Waste and Second Use

What a town throws away, priced as material: recycling bounds, second lives for tyres, batteries and bottles, and the processes that do not survive their own arithmetic.

A Two-Stage Manual Sorting Line for a Small Materials Recovery Shed: Picker Throughput, Belt Coverage Saturation, and a Sized Design for 20 Tonnes per Day

The result. 8 pickers at 0.30 m/s recover 6.39 t/day (50.7% of targets) for $1,088/day revenue vs $1,408/day picker labour; saturation requires ~27 pickers but belt holds 20.

A Replacement Bound for Crushed Concrete as Aggregate: Adhered Mortar, Water Absorption, Strength, and the Price of Washing

The result. Structural replacement bound 28.3% unwashed, 37.4% washed at $12.16/usable tonne; road base 100%; high-strength is a clean kill.

The Glass Cullet Bound: Furnace Energy Keeps Falling to 100 Per Cent Cullet, but Contamination Sets the Usable Limit at One Third

The result. Net energy falls monotonically to 4.395 GJ/t at 100% cullet (17.2% saving), but ceramic contamination sets the usable cullet bound at f=0.33 and the village line cannot make flint-grade cullet.

Landfill Gas Wellfield Spacing: A Saturation Bound from First-Order Generation and Pressure Diffusion, and an Honest Bound on Powering a Town

The result. Capture saturates at ~40 m spacing (91%); 50 m design captures 78.6% of 33.5M m3 CH4 over 30 yr, averaging 0.26 MW = 2.2% of town demand (~214 homes).

Incinerator Bottom Ash as Aggregate: Leaching Against Washing Cycles and Carbonation Ageing, the Water and Salt Burden, and the Uses That Never Qualify

The result. 3 washes + 12 weeks qualifies bound road base and 5 washes + 12 weeks qualifies bound non-structural (Sb controlling); unbound residential never qualifies at any wash count.

How Many Times Can Paper Fibre Be Recycled? A Grade-by-Grade Bound on Fibre Shortening, Fines Loss, Virgin Top-Up and Deinking Cost

The result. Maximum same-grade life is 5 cycles (OCC) down to 2 (newsprint); steady state needs 12-28% virgin top-up per cycle; deinked pulp costs $238-$343/t vs $480-$720/t virgin.

Aluminium Remelt Loss Is a Geometry Problem: A Surface-Area Bound, the Briquetting Gain for Chips, and a Clean Kill for Direct Foil Remelting

The result. Melt loss rises with surface-area-to-volume ratio (1.02% ingot, 13.87% cans, 45% foil capped); briquetting chips nets +$143.48/t, and direct foil remelting at $1,900/t scrap is a clean net loss.

Solar Evaporation of Landfill Leachate: A Clean Kill in Humid Climates and a Narrow Survivor in Arid Ones

The result. CLEAN KILL in humid climates: net evaporation -0.11 m/yr, no finite pond works; evaporation removes 0% of salt mass (104 kg/day remains); arid survivor needs 4,313 m2 for 20 m3/day.

Compost Heat Recovery: The Extraction Rate at Which Cooling a Pile Slows Its Own Decomposition

The result. Trade bound 3.75 kW for a 20 t pile: 21.54 days >=55C, 0.9951 decomposition retention, 5,400 kWh per batch; at 6 kW the thermophilic window is zero days.

Cotton–Polyester Blends: A Second-Use Bound on Mechanical Recycling Cycles and Chemical Separation

The result. 50/50 blend allows 1 good mechanical re-spin; chemical separation loses $2.38-$2.77 per kg and mechanical recycling loses $0.58/kg standalone at stated prices — both chemical routes killed.

Tyre Second Use, Two Routes Priced in One Account: Retreading Keeps a Third of Casings, Pyrolysis Breaks Even Only on Paper It Cannot Cash

The result. Only 372 of 1,000 presented casings (37.2%) are retread-eligible (each retread saves 2,250 MJ and $335); pyrolysis is energy-positive but margins -$2.14/t with 157.5 kg char per tonne still landfilled.

Second Life for Retired Electric-Vehicle Batteries: A Derating Model, a Pack-Level Screening Bound, and the Service Actually Bought

The result. A pack retired at 75% state of health buys 8.56 calendar-limited years of stationary service (not the 19.93 years its cycle budget suggests), and only 18.7% of a stated 1,000-pack retired population passes the derived screening bound for direct reuse.

The result. Oxygen penetrates 0.1949 m at peak respiration; a 2.0 m windrow is 0.8146 anaerobic, core O2 lasts 0.34 h after turning, and a passive chimney supplies only 0.1005 of peak demand (failure printed).

The result. 500 kg/day plant: 43.1 m3 reactor, 47.78 m3 CH4/day, cooking for ~119 households, $2,484/yr digestate value; breaks even at 21.6% contamination and loses money above it.

The PET Melt-Cycle Bound: How Many Bottle-to-Bottle Lives a Polymer Chain Survives, and Whether Restoring It Beats the Cascade

The result. PET survives 3 bottle cycles well-dried, 2 typical, 1 poorly dried; SSP restoration costs 1.091 MJ/kg, 0.0156 of virgin embodied energy, and wins on energy over the cascade.

Lead-Acid Battery Recycling Without the Poisoning: An Exposure Bound for Informal Smelting and a Sealed Retort Design With Its Capture Requirement Derived

The result. Informal smelting exposure is bounded at 310,000× the occupational limit and respirators cannot close the gap (6,200× remaining at protection factor 50); a sealed retort must capture 99.9923% and the specified design models 99.998%.

The result. Eight separation passes are optimal (the ninth returns $217 against $310 cost), netting $17,916 per tonne; small-scale hydrometallurgy loses $1,390 per tonne on reagents alone at the stated 250 g/t gold grade and breaks even only above 268 g/t.
