Household Pests and Stored-Goods Protection

Pests in the house and in the store: traps, baits, meshes and heat, each sized by the insect's own behaviour.

Bait Stations Along an Ant Trail: The Spacing at Which Discovery Time, Not Dose, Becomes the Limit

The result. Place liquid bait stations about 2 m apart along the active trail (nine stations for an 18 m trail, about USD 9.90 in hardware), refill weekly, do not spray the trail during baiting, and allow ten days before judging the attempt; on the stated assumptions the colony dose is complete in about 2.8 days at this spacing, and wider spacings fail mainly by delaying discovery.

The Thick Mattress Problem: Heat-Soak Time Scales With the Square of Thickness in Bed Bug Room Treatments

The result. Plan the hold from the thickest item: at 60 C air, allow at least 4 hours for 10 cm of foam, 16 hours for 20 cm, and treat 30 cm items (36 hours) as impractical to clear in place — split, open or remove thick items so no conduction path exceeds about 10 cm, and verify with a probe in the core of the thickest remaining item.

One Trap or Two? Detection Probability for a Light Clothes Moth Infestation in a Wool Store

The result. Hang two pheromone traps per small textile store room, at low level and at opposite ends, replace them every eight weeks (USD 12 per round), and treat any catch as a trigger to inspect and treat the textiles themselves; a third trap in the same room adds cost without detection.

Gel Dots on a Grid: The Widest Spacing That Still Collapses a German Cockroach Population in a Kitchen

The result. Apply gel dots of about 0.1 g at no more than 1.1 m spacing along every harbourage line, counting each cabinet level separately (19 dots for 20 m of line, under USD 1 of gel), and re-apply at the same spacing after about three weeks; spacings of 3 m or more should be expected to fail outright, however much gel each dot contains.

Warm Washes and Dust Mites: The Temperature Below Which No Practical Cycle Reaches a Three-Log Reduction

The result. Wash bedding intended as a mite control measure at 60 C with a normal-length cycle (about 24 minutes of allowance covers the 3-log target at the load's coldest point); treat 40 C washing as allergen-removal cleaning only, and use hot tumble drying, freezing or encasements for items that cannot be washed at 60 C.

Vacuuming Against the Flea Life Cycle: The Interval Below Which a Carpet Reservoir Collapses Without Insecticide

The result. Vacuum the pet's resting and sleeping areas daily for the first 14 days of a flea infestation and every second day for the following 14, alongside effective treatment of the pet; weekly vacuuming should be expected to leave a breeding reservoir in place indefinitely.

Sieving Infested Flour: The Aperture That Removes the Adults and Leaves the Eggs, and the Finer Mesh That Still Leaves Most of Them

The result. Never treat sieving as a cure for infested flour: use a sieve of about 0.45 mm to inspect and to prepare flour for a freezing or heating step, expect it to leave roughly four eggs in five behind, and discard flour whose visible catch suggests contamination near 100 individuals per kilogram.

One Vinegar Trap Does Not Clear a Fruit Fly Outbreak: A Growth-and-Capture Disproof for Kitchens

The result. Do not rely on a vinegar trap to clear an outbreak while any breeding site remains; clean or remove the site first (drains, fruit, bins, spills), then run one or two traps for a fortnight as mop-up and as indicators of a missed site.

Paper · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24121601

Cooling Grain With Night Air: Why Bin Diameter Drops Out of the Aeration Time and Airflow Rate Does Not

The result. Budget about 29 nights of eight cool, dry hours to bring a wheat bulk from 28 C to a 15 C mean at 5 m3/h per tonne, whatever the bin diameter; halve the airflow rate only if twice as many nights are available, and double it if only a fortnight of suitable nights remains.

How Long Until a Sealed Grain Bag Runs Low on Oxygen? Infestation Density Sets the Clock in Hermetic Storage

The result. Keep a hermetic bag sealed for at least five weeks when the grain went in with a light infestation (around 2 adult-equivalents per kg), at least a fortnight at moderate density, and treat any opening as resetting the protection clock to zero; hold grain for weekly use in a separate small bag.

Wet Combing for Head Lice: Four Passes, Four Sessions — A Schedule Set by the Egg, Not the Adult

The result. Wet-comb with a 0.22 mm comb in four full passes per session on days 1, 5, 9 and 13 (about 25 minutes per session), checking close contacts on the same days; do not judge the treatment by the first session, and protect the day-9 session above all others.

How Far Apart Can Snap Traps Sit? A Wall-Coverage Rule for the House Mouse in Kitchens and Store Rooms

The result. Space snap traps no more than 12 m apart along a confirmed mouse run, concentrate them at wall junctions, corners and the gaps behind appliances, and check and reset them every night; on the stated assumptions this gives a 0.995 capture probability within 48 hours for a single mouse, and extra traps should be justified as redundancy, not coverage.

Forty-Eight Hours in the Freezer: Where the Pantry Moth Rule Holds for Single Bags and Fails for Stacked Flour

The result. Freeze flour and grain in single bags or a single layer for 48 hours at minus 22 C; never rely on 48 hours for a tight stack — allow about 4.5 days for a 40 cm stack or restack it halfway through so that every bag spends time on the outside of the pile.

Paper · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24121624

Twelve-Millimetre Mesh Does Not Exclude Mice: A Passage-Probability Bound for Household Exclusion Work

The result. Proof against mice with mesh or grille apertures of at most 6 mm (about 4 to 5 mm for a strict below-1-percent bound), treat every clear gap over 6 mm as an entry regardless of material around it, and never rely on expanding foam as the barrier layer.

Paper · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24121665

Drying a Store Room Below the Silverfish Threshold: Dehumidifier Capacity From a Moisture Balance

The result. For a roughly 48 m3 basement store room in a humid summer climate, buy a dehumidifier rated at 12 L per day or more, plumb it for continuous drainage, seal the room's air leaks first, and verify with a hygrometer that the room holds at or below 60 percent relative humidity.

Paper · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24121710

Beans in the Sun: Bag Mass, Projected Area, and the Hours Needed to Reach 50 Degrees in a Black Enclosure

The result. Solar-treat beans in a single layer in bags of at most 25 kg where possible (about 4.7 hours including a one-hour hold in strong sun), split 50 kg sacks first, turn bags at the midpoint, and verify 50 C at the core of the largest bag before storing.

Paper · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24121651

Stations Around a House Perimeter: Termite Interception Probability as a Function of Spacing

The result. Space subterranean termite bait stations about 3 m apart around the perimeter (15 stations for a 44 m house), inspect them quarterly, and reject layouts wider than 4 m as materially weaker; beyond 6 m the interception probability falls below three chances in four per season.
