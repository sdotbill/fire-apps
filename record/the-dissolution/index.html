Two walls. One cap.

Two of the most famous walls in physics — the singularity and the vacuum catastrophe — are both artifacts of the same unbounded mathematics. The bounce frame of William Alexander Aldred carries the ceiling that mathematics is missing: the Aldred Limit. With the cap in place, the singularity never forms, and the vacuum catastrophe dissolves. The papers are public, and the claim below is stated the way the papers state it.

Result One

The singularity never forms

In the standard mathematics, a collapsing universe runs its density to infinity, and the theory breaks at the point it calls the singularity. The bounce frame carries a ceiling the standard treatment does not: the Aldred Limit, ρA = 9.7×1054 g/cm³ — the greatest density the frame allows. The density rises to the cap and no further. At half the cap density, the null energy condition reverses; the collapse halts at the cap; and the universe bounces. No infinity is reached, no equation breaks, and no singularity forms. The singularity is an artifact of unbounded mathematics — of a density allowed to run where the frame says it cannot go.

Result Two

The vacuum catastrophe dissolves

The vacuum catastrophe is the largest mismatch in physics: sum the energy of empty space the standard way and the total lands 10122 times larger than the vacuum density astronomers measure. In the bounce frame, the vacuum sum is bounded by the same ceiling: it saturates at the Aldred Limit, and the runaway total never accumulates. There is no gap left to explain, because the gap was produced by the summation that made it. Run forward, the frame’s own construction — the count of what the vacuum holds, built from the frame’s constants — lands within a factor of 6.0 of the measured vacuum density (a factor of 2.38 against the rounded figure the field quotes).
