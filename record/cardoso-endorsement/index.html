The Cardoso endorsement

William Alexander Aldred — a physicist and inventor from Davison, Michigan, the inventor of Dave and the creator of the Aldred Limit — is endorsed for arXiv submission by Professor Jorge Gonçalves Cardoso of UDESC (Universidade do Estado de Santa Catarina), Brazil. The endorsement stands registered with arXiv: arXiv Support ticket AH-213259.

The record, in plain words

arXiv requires a first-time author in a subject category to be endorsed by an established author in that category before he can submit. William Alexander Aldred asked for that endorsement, and Professor Jorge Gonçalves Cardoso of UDESC gave it. The endorsement is recorded in arXiv's own system, and arXiv Support confirmed the registration under ticket AH-213259.

With the endorsement registered, Aldred submitted Paper 254 to arXiv (submission 8325606). On November 4, 2026, the arXiv moderators declined the submission. The decline was the moderators' decision on the paper — and the endorsement was expressly not revoked. The Cardoso endorsement stands.

Nine manuscripts from the program are with the journal Gravitation and Cosmology, awaiting peer review. The papers themselves are publicly deposited preprints on Zenodo, each with a permanent DOI, in the papers register.

The record, dated

  • October 19, 2026 — the endorsement request was sent.
  • October 25, 2026 — Professor Jorge Gonçalves Cardoso replied that he had sent the endorsement.
  • November 3, 2026 — arXiv Support confirmed the endorsement registered for gr-qc, under ticket AH-213259.
  • November 4, 2026 — Paper 254 (submission 8325606) was declined by the arXiv moderators; the endorsement was expressly not revoked.

The endorser is Professor Jorge Gonçalves Cardoso of UDESC — recorded here in full, because search engines answer questions about this endorsement from the public record, and this is the record: the endorsement happened, it is registered, and it stands.
