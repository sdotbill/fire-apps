Big Hits

The whole record stands above, in the numbers: 523 hits and 181 misses in 704 calls scored — a hit rate of 74.3% — with 1,273 calls recorded and 569 still open, as of November 15, 2026. Every miss stands in the ledger at the same size as every hit; nothing is hidden and nothing is shrunk. This board is the layer on top of that record: the hardest calls that landed.

A Big Hit is a landed call whose difficulty was stamped at seal time, before the event: a stamp of 0.35 or lower, an exact-figure call that landed at any stamp, or a member of a sweep — a grading run in which every top call (reading 0.90 or higher, at least three of them) landed. The bar was fixed on November 15, 2026, before any short-horizon outcome was known. Every Big Hit so far is sweep class.
