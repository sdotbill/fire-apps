Big Hits

The whole record stands above, in the numbers: 523 hits and 181 misses in 704 calls scored — a hit rate of 74.3% — with 1,273 calls recorded and 569 still open, as of November 15, 2026. Every miss stands in the ledger at the same size as every hit; nothing is hidden and nothing is shrunk. This board is the layer on top of that record: the hardest calls that landed.

A Big Hit is a landed call whose difficulty was stamped at seal time, before the event: a stamp of 0.35 or lower, an exact-figure call that landed at any stamp, or a call sealed at the desk's top confidence — a stamp of 0.90 or higher — that landed. The bar was fixed on November 15, 2026, before any short-horizon outcome was known. One hundred twenty-six Big Hits have landed so far: 125 top-confidence class and one exact class.

How to read this board

Every entry is a call from the desk's ledger, identified by its seal number — the PD number. Every call was sealed before the event: the seal is the receipt.

  • Difficulty stamp — how likely the call was to land, recorded at seal time, before anyone knew the result. A stamp of 0.977 means the call was a heavy favorite, expected to land about 98 times in 100. A stamp near 0.50 is a coin flip. A stamp of 0.35 or lower is a long shot.
  • Top-confidence class — the call was sealed at a difficulty stamp of 0.90 or higher — the desk's top confidence — and it landed. Every call stands on its own.
  • Exact class — the call named an exact figure — a rate, a score, a number — and the outcome matched it exactly.
  • Long-shot class — a call stamped 0.35 or lower that landed anyway: a genuine underdog that came in.
