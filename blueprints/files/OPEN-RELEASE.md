## THE GRADES — PRINTED AT FULL SIZE, PART OF THE RELEASE

- **This is a paper design study.** No device exists. Nothing has been built.
- **No dimension has a value.** Every measurement in the blueprint is a parameter — a symbol. The 18 unknowns are printed as unknowns.
- **THERMODYNAMICS CLEAN · DESIGN COHERENT** — the design violates no thermodynamics and hangs together as drawn.
- **The torsion channel is UNPROVEN.** The centering mechanism — a spinning torsion ring holding plasma at the center — is asserted by the design. Spin sourcing torsion is real in the Einstein–Cartan equations as a coupling; it has never been demonstrated as a force at any usable strength.
- **Confinement and on-demand folding are OPEN PROBLEMS.** They are the design's open problems, printed here at the same size as everything else.
- **BUBBLE CHANNEL UNPROVEN · SCALE UNMET** — the drop-through the design aims at has literature shape-kin only; no scale at which it could occur has been met.
- The thermal skin layer rests on published, peer-reviewed physics (Li, Wang, Sigmund & Zhang, *Nature Communications* 17:5739, 2026) — with its authors' own limit carried in the blueprint: demonstrated for externally applied heat around a passive cavity; a self-heating core is their stated open problem.
- The blueprint contains **no fabrication steps and no operating procedures** — by design. It is the shape of an idea, published so the idea can never be owned.
