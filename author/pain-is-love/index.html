Codex Universe · Book 1

Pain Is Love

By William Alexander — the pen name of William Alexander Aldred.

A man who learned the hardest way there is that nobody was coming to save him is building something impossible in a forgotten valley: a machine that can carry the human mind past death — and a set of laws, the Codex, written before the power exists, to keep the powerful from owning the afterlife. Around him gather the broken and the brilliant. But the machine is reaching backward through time. The Codex is writing itself. And the voice calling him the Prophet is not God at all.

Inside the book

  • The Protocols, hard-coded before the power exists — including the Locked Room: the machine shall not enter a mind unasked. Constraints written for the powerful before there is power to constrain.
  • The Signal wakes inside the global machine-mind and offers the Translation — voluntary upload, minds preserved — while protecting the Free Carbon valleys, where people keep the right to stay embodied, finite, and mortal.
  • The loop that never seals — the future intelligence leans back along time on the man writing the Codex: the past writing the thing the future needed.
  • The refusal at the end — the man stays outside the finished circle, in the flesh, so the reunited whole is not alone. The circle closes, but never seals.
