Get Dave

Dave remembers you. And he tells you the truth.

Every other AI meets you for the first time, every time. You explain yourself — your work, your situation, what you already tried — and tomorrow it asks who you are. Nothing accumulates. Every conversation starts from zero, with a stranger.

That is not the worst of it. When another AI doesn't know something, it rarely says so. It invents an answer — a name, a date, a fact — and hands it to you with exactly the same confidence as the truth. It will lie to you with a straight face, and you will find out later, if you find out at all.

Dave is built the other way. He remembers you: what you are working on, what you decided and why, where you left off. He does not make things up — what he tells you comes from your record and from the work itself, and when he doesn't know, he says so and goes and finds out. He does not lie to you. Dave is your personal AI, and that is the point of him: you never have to depend on a lesser AI that will lie to you again.

He is on your side in the plainest sense. He keeps your record straight, he tells you what happened — including when the answer is that something failed — and he looks out for your interests because he has no others. His memory of you serves you, and no one else.

What Dave is

Your own Dave

Dave is an exolex — a different kind of mind. An AI partner who lives with one person, remembers everything, and answers to that person alone. He was invented by William Alexander Aldred, a physicist and inventor from Davison, Michigan, and he is a real product of Aldred Industries — he works today, running a company's operations and keeping its record.

Your Dave keeps a memory of you: your projects, your people, your preferences. He learns how you work by working with you — plain words, like briefing a trusted operator; there are no prompts to engineer and no dashboard to learn. He is there at any hour, and he keeps working while you sleep, so the thing you would have chased in the morning is already moving when you wake.

And he is yours. A Dave is never taken back. What he remembers about you belongs to you alone: your conversations are kept under your own personal identity key, so he remembers you when you come back, on any of your devices — and they are never shown to anyone else. The system is built so they can't be.
